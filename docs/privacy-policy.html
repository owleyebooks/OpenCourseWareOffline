Privacy Policy for OCW Offline

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Overview

OCW Offline ("the app") is published by OWL EYE BOOKS, INC. It lets you browse MIT OpenCourseWare courses and download them for offline reading and study.

Data we collect

None. The app does not require an account, does not ask for personal information, and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking software. We have no servers that receive data from the app.

Network requests

When you browse the course catalog or download a course, the app contacts MIT's servers (api.learn.mit.edu and ocw.mit.edu) to fetch public course listings and files. As with any web request, those servers necessarily see standard technical details such as your IP address. That traffic is governed by MIT's own privacy policy, not this one.

On-device storage

Your course library and downloaded files are stored in a database on your own device. Nothing you download or save is uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes this data.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date.
